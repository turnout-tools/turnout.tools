Insights on how we've seen different audiences on different platforms, and which ones do better than others and at what categories.

## Audience Personas by Platform

### 1. Lu.ma & Offlyn (Tech, Startups, Creators)
*   **Demographics**: 18–35 years old, professional, tech-adjacent.
*   **Intent**: Networking, learning, high-value workshops.

### 2. District & Putting Scene (Subculture, Music, Art)
*   **Demographics**: 18–30 years old, highly active social scene.
*   **Intent**: Music gigs, community meetups, unique experiences, art showcases.

### 3. BookMyShow (Mainstream Entertainment)
*   **Demographics**: Broad mainstream audience (families, casual goers).
*   **Intent**: Movies, comedy specials, large-scale music concerts.
