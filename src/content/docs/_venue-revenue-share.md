Why revenue share is better than rentals, and why we don't want to ever do minimum guarantees (MGs).

## Incentive Alignment Comparison

### Scenario A: Flat Rental
*   **Venue Risk**: Low. They get paid regardless of turnout.
*   **Organizer Risk**: High. If turnout is low, the organizer loses money.
*   **Cooperation**: Low. Venue has no incentive to promote the event.

### Scenario B: Revenue Share (Recommended)
*   **Venue Risk**: Shared.
*   **Organizer Risk**: Shared.
*   **Cooperation**: High. Both parties are incentivized to fill the venue.

---

## The Rule on Minimum Guarantees
> [!CAUTION]
> **Avoid Minimum Guarantees**
> 
> Minimum Guarantees (MGs) shift all downside risk back onto the organizer while capping the upside. Avoid agreements with venues that require minimum bar spend or ticket revenue guarantees.
