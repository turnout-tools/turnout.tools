## Insights on Indian Ticket Buying Behavior
*   **The Procrastination Window**: Up to 60-70% of tickets for non-mega events are purchased in the final 4 days before the event.
*   **The Early Bird Trigger**: Early bird tiers drive validation and initial traction, usually representing 15-20% of capacity.
*   **Payment Reminders**: Sending reminders on paydays (e.g., 1st or 30th/31st of the month) dramatically boosts transaction rates.
