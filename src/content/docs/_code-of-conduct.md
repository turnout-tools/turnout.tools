## Why have a Code of Conduct (CoC)?
Many places have a code of conduct but it's hidden away in T&Cs, or only brought up to resolve conflicts.

The purpose of a CoC is a bit more than that, it's also a way to signal to attendees and partners what your values are, and that you are proactive in trying to maintain a healthy, inclusive community.

All that said, there no point in having a CoC if it's never surfaced to attendees and partners in the first place.

Having this as a part of your ticketing flow is difficult because most platforms don't let you customise the description with links, or include links in email/sms/whatsapp confirmations.

The more practical way to surface it online is:
- A pinned post on your Instagram, or a highlight
- A link in your Instagram profile and WhatsApp community description

Ultimately, the best place to do this is in person. If your event has a host/MC, have them mention this as part of the welcome/introduction. If there is a presentation, include a slide with a summary and QR code for the full one.
