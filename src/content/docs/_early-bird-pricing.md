Why we do early bird pricing and how to implement it effectively.

## Objectives of Early Bird Tiers

1.  **Validation**: Verifies market interest early in the production cycle.
2.  **Social Proof**: A "Sold Out" badge on early bird tiers builds urgency for standard tiers.
3.  **Cash Flow**: Secures early capital to pay event booking advances or marketing costs.

---

## Best Practices
*   **Cap by Quantity, not Time**: A time-limited tier doesn't trigger urgency like seeing "Only 5 left at this price!".
*   **Discount Depth**: Keep early bird pricing 20% to 35% lower than the final door price.
*   **Strict Transitions**: Never extend early bird sales; doing so breaks trust and dilutes future ticket campaigns.
