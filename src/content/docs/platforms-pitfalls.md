## Platform Listing Traps & Best Practices

### 1. Hidden Payment Gateway Fees
*   **The Pitfall**: Thinking the commission fee is the only cost, only to discover a 2.5% PG fee is added at checkout.
*   **The Fix**: Explicitly verify with platform support if PG charges are integrated into the base fee or billed separately.

### 2. Slow Payout Cycles
*   **The Pitfall**: Assuming ticketing revenues will pay for venue rentals/deposits upfront, only to find the platform retains funds until 7 days post-event.
*   **The Fix**: Maintain emergency reserves or choose platforms offering T+1 or T+3 payout cycles.
